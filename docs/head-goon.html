What changed with images

The extra input was one screenshot from the same frame as the structured observation, enlarged from 160×144 to 640×576 without smoothing. The model still received the boss phase and timers, ball state and approximate physics forecasts.

Adding screenshots improved OpenAI's results in this sample, even alongside structured game state. Jev still completed more attempts. The sample does not establish a reliable image advantage.

Head Goon methods and limitations
  • All 90 selected attempts used the same contact-rule ROM and arena checkpoint. The first five image-assisted pilot runs are excluded.
  • The goal was to hit Head Goon three times during tray-down recovery, then reach the exit alive. One physical wool ball had to be retrieved.
  • Each run had its own delay estimate, starting at ten frames and updating with 0.65 old / 0.35 new weights. Previous controls remained held during API calls; the minimum interval after applying an answer was six frames.
  • The three setups were collected in successive batches. Some batches were interleaved, but the complete comparison was not a single contemporaneous randomized experiment. Network conditions differed.
  • More attempts were requested after earlier results were inspected. Confidence intervals do not adjust for those repeated looks. The exploratory two-sided Fisher comparison of OpenAI text and image gives p = 0.103 before multiple-comparison adjustment.
  • No retries, fallback controls or replacement failures. All selected replays passed framebuffer and game-state verification.
