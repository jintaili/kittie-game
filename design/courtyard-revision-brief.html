Kittie Has Other Plans · Courtyard design brief · 2 October 2026

Carry something worth keeping.

The courtyard is a walled approach to the wedding grounds. Kittie learns to use one ball, crosses the service gardens, and arrives at the last bell with a ball he has kept safe. The existing jumps and patrols already ask enough of the player. Give those actions consequences and give the place a stronger shape.

Three physical balls for the whole courtyard. Put one on the opening path and two on optional upper ledges. Remove the guaranteed supply at both bells and before the patrols. Balls still survive hits and must be retrieved. Reaching the last bell without any means retrying the stage with Start.

Exact changes

Keep the stage at 1280 by 144 pixels, with ground at y128. Keep both bells, both gates, all three patrol ranges, all three 32-pixel water gaps, and the existing jump and damage rules. Add just one climbing choice and move the final commitment.

The first two screens contain no water, so learning the first bell normally risks time rather than losing a ball. The middle stretch has no free replacement. An upper-route ball matters because it can rescue a missed throw into a channel. The final court has dry ground and walls for retrieval, but it cannot rescue an empty inventory.

Less writing, better stonework

Keep A JUMP B THROW once near the start and UP+B near the first bell. Remove the other world captions, including the table explanation and exit label. Do not replace them with a row of arrows. A continuous wall, visible gaps, the bell cords and the 14-pixel table passage should explain the space.

Replace the house repeated every 64 pixels with broad warm stucco walls, a few recessed arches and shared stone trims. Use terracotta on roof fragments, olive on foliage, and cooler darks in recesses. Keep the darkest outline for solid ledges, water lips and working objects. Reuse window, coping and leaf modules; leave large areas of plaster plain. Reserve pink for balls so each ball is immediately recognizable.

What would make this fail

The new shelf must reward an intentional second jump while allowing the lower path. A clean run must finish with the opening ball alone. Missing a throw near water should make recovery or the upper reserve matter. Entering the last court empty must remain an honest failed attempt, with the bell visible before commitment. The table shot must remain recoverable after a miss.

Apply the reduced active supply to the courtyard only. The grove and banquet retain their current six balls and rules. Preserve stage unlocks, cover v10, Kittie's approved sprite, back carry and idle cuddle. This brief does not change his face or body.
