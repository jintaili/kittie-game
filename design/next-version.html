Recommendations retained from the discussion

Build depth and replay value in the existing stages before expanding the level count. Treat wool as something Kittie likes to keep. Scoring rewards play; a shop, currency and upgrade economy have not been approved. Examples such as an irrigation wheel, table dishes or a branch shortcut were suggestions, not commitments to build every one.

Wool retains its two gameplay uses, ringing bells and defeating goons. Preserve the scarcity of physical balls and stage retries. Difficulty may lean slightly high. Avoid adding explanatory text when the action, sound or picture can communicate the same thing.

Pilot choices

The team selected 100 points per unique goon defeated in the current attempt and 200 per ball carried through the courtyard exit, for a maximum of 900. Retrying clears the attempt. Only a completed score can replace the saved best, and a lower score cannot reduce it. The existing completion overlay will show the result and best. The other stages keep their current scoring behavior.

The last courtyard goon moves onto the final table. A flat throw passes beneath it to the bell, while a jumping throw can target the patrol. The patrol keeps its constant speed. Gaps, wool supply and core movement stay as shipped. This is the team's pilot design, subject to play review.

The pilot also includes a small environmental animation pass, starting with irrigation-water ripples. Keep stone banks and collision cues stationary. Other ambient movement remains approved for later iterations, rather than being rejected or postponed as a whole.

The independent critic cleared the full level with 700 points and two hearts, judged it worth playing, and requested one correction: a successful low shot could strand wool beneath the final table. The bell now sits twenty pixels nearer the approach. Its existing physical rebound brings the wool back within reach. The table, guard and ball physics remain unchanged.

The revised native and web cartridges both reached the 900-point maximum with three hearts and three retained balls using the ordinary low shot. The independent critic passed the correction. The verifier confirmed published-v1 unlock import and the final 900-point best through a cold restart. The pilot is ready for human play.
