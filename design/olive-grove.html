The olive grove

Eleven screens built around broad raised stone islands and irrigation channels. There is no opening bell court. An optional high path carries wool above a lower patrol lane, followed by one enclosed bell garden.

Version 1 guide · Playable ROM

Scroll to inspect the full stage. Pink circles are wool; plum circles and lines are constant-speed patrols; blue strips are fatal water; gold marks are bells. Purple bars are bell gates. The pale tall bar is the closing court shutter. Vertical guides mark 160-pixel screens.

Rules that carry across stages

Collect up to three balls. B throws low; Up+B lobs. Balls defeat goons and ring bells. There is no automatic return, recall or jump boost. Goon contact costs a heart. Water or losing every heart resets the current stage. Start retries; Select returns to the stage menu. Clearing a stage saves the next unlock.

Native playtests reached the ending. The grove’s upper and lower routes passed separately. Its upper ledge ends early enough to leave the lower water-jump takeoff clear. Both grove and banquet final courts were completed after entering empty.
