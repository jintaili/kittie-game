Play

Left and Right move. A jumps; hold it for a higher jump. B throws a ball, and Up+B lobs it. Touch a resting ball to collect it. Start retries the current stage; Select returns to the cover. A or Start opens the stage selector, and B closes it.

Each stage has three wool balls and Kittie starts with three hearts. Water or losing all hearts resets the whole stage. A ball does not return automatically. It rings bells and defeats goons.

Score

The score stays at the top. Each defeated goon earns 100 points; completing a stage adds 200 for each ball still held, up to 900 total. Each stage saves its own best. Retrying discards the attempt, and a lower completed score cannot replace a higher best.
