Kittie Has Other Plans · Wedding banquet design brief · 2 October 2026

Across the feast, into the photograph.

The finale should ask Kittie to use the furniture. Enter beneath a table, climb across the feast, handle a patrol on the tabletop, then make the last shot from the cake table. The wedding arch earns a quiet final screen after the work is done.

Keep the nine-screen length and existing jumps. Put the second patrol on the long feast table, raise the final bell, and reduce the supply to three physical balls. Leave the final court open behind Kittie. The challenge comes from crossing the hall with a ball still available.

Make the supply honest

The opening table has a 14-pixel passage for a ball. A prior verified run left the first ball beneath it at x203.875, beyond Kittie's pickup reach. Completion must therefore work without recovering that ball. The x576 pickup restores the ability to throw after the climb; the x984 pickup provides a late reserve. Neither is replaced automatically.

Keep the last court open so the player can collect a spare left behind or retrieve balls that land outside it. Losing every usable ball can still end the attempt and require Start. Do not add an emergency ball. This arrangement makes supply matter without making an unseen shutter decide the run.

A wedding hall with a destination

Remove all five world captions. Replace the window repeated every 80 pixels with a few paired windows and generous plaster gaps. Concentrate candles, plates and cloth detail around the central feast, then simplify the room toward the arch. Use a thin dark edge for actual tabletops and softer colors for rear architecture. Shallow cloth hems can describe the raised tables; decorative legs must not pretend to block playable passages. Solid sideboards should look solid. Keep the familiar blue channel cue and reserve pink for balls.

The ending can retain one KITTIE MADE IT heading and a compact THANKS FOR PLAYING message. Preserve its return-to-title behavior, saved completion, and exact approved Kittie artwork. Do not revise the cover or character sprite. See the independent art review for native presentation evidence.

What the built level must prove

A complete run must succeed after leaving the opening ball under its table. The raised patrol must allow a readable landing before contact, while requiring a decision to cross its table. The late reserve must be reachable and the final landing must remain fair at different patrol phases. Verify the elevated bell from the actual cake table, including a recoverable miss. An empty final court must allow retreat, and losing all usable balls must remain a deliberate retry. Recheck the wedding ending and saved completion after the full run.

Scope is the banquet and its closing presentation. Preserve the revised courtyard, grove, cover v10 and approved character assets. Based on native source inspection and existing evidence; the new shot and patrol timing still need the builder's actual-ROM playtest.
