Designer brief · 2 October 2026 · 11 screens · 1760 × 144 pixels

The olive grove

Kittie climbs the irrigation terraces, chooses between high branches and a patrolled orchard floor, then makes one careful crossing before the bell garden. Three physical wool balls serve the whole journey. Early fights reward retrieving a throw; the high route offers a spare. Keep the current jump and throw physics.

The difficulty increase comes from shorter island tops, a longer branch sequence and one 64-pixel channel. Keep landing areas visible and give each patrol safe ground for recovering balls. The final garden stays open behind Kittie.

Ball and the bell garden

Use exactly three active balls at 56,124, 808,44 and 1504,100. Remove the other three pickups. Each ball stays where the current physics puts it, and water loss remains permanent until retry. Retain three hearts, constant-speed goons, whole-stage resets and manual Start retry.

Move the last bell to 1584,56. Keep its gate at 1640 and the court camera at 1576, starting at 1508. The perch ends at 1544, leaving a dry recovery lane beneath the bell. Set door_index to 255 and remove the shutter rectangle at 1440. Keep the first gate already open.

The bell's 12-pixel hit radius matters. At y64, a standing high lob from the floor can still ring it. At y56, Kittie earns the perch or jumps. Do not tighten hitboxes or alter the shared throw.

Native presentation

Remove all three grove world captions. Use two or three reusable, asymmetric olive canopy groups. Place trunks beside the route and leave platform ends, ball and the bell clear. The branch route should read as olive wood, while the raised islands read as masonry. Keep dark walkable top edges consistent. Use a few joined leaf clusters and restrained stone joints; replace the current repeated flecks and identical tree spacing. Keep the bell cable legible against open sky. Coordinate the final palette work with the independent art review.

Edit only grove geometry, its native background and palette data, then regenerate the shared level data. Keep the approved courtyard, banquet, Kittie, cover, title controls and saved unlocks intact.
