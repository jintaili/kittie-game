Kittie's garden action level

Implemented prototype, 1 October 2026. Eight screens, 1,280 × 144 pixels. A complete path to the Tuscan wedding photo, with the middle rebuilt around wool-ball combat and two routes.

Level profile

Stone blocks are solid terrain, purple circles are goons, blue strips are channels, and the golden dot is the optional ribbon. Vertical guides mark screen widths.

Rules and feel

The ball has exactly two uses: ring bells and knock away goons. Low bat and lob share the B button; hold Up for a lob. There is no ball-assisted bounce. Goons patrol, pause for 26 updates, charge, then recover. One moving-ball hit knocks a goon away. Contact bumps Kittie back and briefly makes him invulnerable.

Jump input is buffered for six updates, with six updates of grace after leaving an edge. Deep water returns Kittie to the latest flag. Progress through gates, enemies and the ribbon survives a fall. There are no lives or time limit. Lost or abandoned wool returns nearby in the garden, with Select available for a manual reset.

Color and character

Warm plaster, terracotta roofs, olive foliage, limestone platforms and blue channels give the garden distinct areas. Rose wool and plum goons contrast with Kittie's taupe and chocolate fur. His approved wide face, low torso and short legs are unchanged.

Recorded play

Actual emulator frames from the final ROM. Two complete runs reached the finish: one via the lower combat route, one via the upper ribbon route. The first shallow channel and a deep-water failure were tested. Intermediate failures are retained alongside the successful runs.

This iteration keeps the level's length steady. The next expansion should build on player feedback about combat timing and route choice. Physical Chromatic play and audio listening checks remain outstanding.
