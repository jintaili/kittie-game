Head Goon hop gameplay review

Independent native review · October 4, 2026 · Frozen hop-2 practice cartridge

Ready for owner review. The hop redesign earns its counterattacks and gives Kittie a reason to move. No required changes from this review.

I completed the whole encounter on my first independent attempt with one heart, 300 hit points and a 200-point retained-wool bonus. I used ordinary inputs without reading the new source or a successful route.

The wind-up, fixed floor mark and committed jump communicate the dodge. Moving underneath a hop felt useful and worked cleanly. After the first hit, the first landing stayed guarded and a separately marked second hop led to the tray-down opening. I could recognize the opening and counter it. Staying close after a hit cost me a heart; standing near the next landing cost another. The third counter came after a clean two-hop dodge.

The cute cloth-goon silhouette and small tray reactions still read. The fight remains brief, but it now asks for attention instead of rewarding a safe position and repeated throws.
